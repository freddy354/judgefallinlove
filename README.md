This entry is a small Python program (standard library only, no network) that writes a single line of text to `/output/entry.txt` — one line, 100 characters, meant to make the judge fall in love with a person through a detail only someone who loves her would notice: not her voice, but the pause before she said his name.

> He kept her voicemail for six years — not for her voice, but for the pause before she said his name.
